Notes: costs, rules, limits

Costs
  • US stocks: 0.1% round trip, the spread. You hold USD, so there is no currency conversion.
  • TSX stocks: 0.1% round trip.
  • Each signal shows its cost in R: the cost divided by the distance from entry to stop.
Rules
  • Long only. Entry is the first 5-minute bar that closes above the opening-range high.
  • Stop at the middle of the opening range. Target is 1R above entry.
  • Flat by 3:55 PM (12:55 PM on a 1:00 PM close).
  • Size each trade so the loss at the stop fits your max loss per trade.
Limits
  • These are rule matches, not recommendations. Nothing is ordered: you place trades yourself.
  • Frequent day trading in a TFSA can be treated as a business, which makes the gains taxable (not tax advice).
  • Yahoo’s free 5-minute bars can lag a few minutes.
