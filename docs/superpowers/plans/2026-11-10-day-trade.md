# Day Trade Tab Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A Day trade tab that, every 5 minutes from 9:50 AM to 3:30 PM ET on trading days, finds volume-confirmed opening-range breakouts in US and TSX stocks, plans the stop/target, replays each trade, ranks them after costs, shows the top 3 (or "no trade"), tracks them to the exit and keeps a track record.

**Architecture:** A new pipeline package `advisor/daytrade/` ports the needed parts of the day-trader skill (MIT) into tested modules: Yahoo bars, the rule (range, entry, exit plan), the scan, the replay, scoring, tracking, news, and a cycle loop. A new workflow runs the loop in two jobs a day, started by Supabase pg_cron. Results go to two new tables; the web app reads them on a new `/daytrade` page.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.12 (stdlib urllib for Yahoo, psycopg 3, pytest), Supabase Postgres + RLS + pg_cron/pg_net, GitHub Actions, Next.js App Router, Vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-day-trade-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- The rule is the skill's, unchanged: 15-minute opening range (9:30, 9:35, 9:40 five-minute bars); entry = first completed 5-minute bar after the range that CLOSES above the range high with volume ≥ **1.5×** the range bars' average; one signal per stock per day; stop = opening-range midpoint rounded DOWN to the cent; target = **2R**; flat by **15:55 ET** (12:55 on a 13:00 early close); no new signals after **15:30** (12:30 on an early close). Long only.
- Replay: **10,000** replays, the skill's method (bars drawn from the same time of day ± 2 bars over the stock's last full sessions from a 60-day 5-minute history), fixed seed per (date, symbol) so reruns match; history = the same rule on each past full session.
- Cost a round trip (%): US = 2 × `settings.usd_fx_fee_pct` + `settings.daytrade_spread_pct`; TSX = `settings.daytrade_spread_pct`. `daytrade_spread_pct` default **0.10**. Cost in R = cost% / 100 × entry / (entry − stop).
- Eligible = history fired ≥ **20** times AND net history R > 0 AND net replay R > 0. Rank eligible by net history R, ties by net replay R. Top 3 = ranks 1–3. Fresh = within **10 minutes** of the trigger bar's close.
- Full universe scan at 9:50 and every **15 minutes** after; cycles between re-check only the day's candidates. A cycle starts 30 s after each 5-minute bar closes; a long cycle skips missed bars.
- Two jobs a day: morning **9:35–12:40 ET**, afternoon **12:35–15:59 ET**; the afternoon job finalizes at 15:55 (open signals → flat at that bar's close).
- Outcome when a bar touches both stop and target: **stop** (conservative). Stop/target hits use bar lows/highs after the entry bar.
- No orders, no notifications. Display only. Picks/Invest/forecasts/model untouched.
- Yahoo: User-Agent `Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; stock-advisor; +https://github.com/capak07/stock-advisor)`; query2 first, query1 on 429/5xx; one retry after 5 s on a 429; 0.3 s pause between batch requests; spark ≤ 20 symbols a request.
- Credit the skill (MIT, github.com/Jakeschincariol/day-trader-skill) in each ported module's docstring. Copied fixtures go under `pipeline/tests/fixtures/daytrade/` with a README naming the source and license.
- All SQL in `pipeline/src/advisor/store/repo.py`; web data only in `web/lib/data/*.ts` ('server-only'); writes from the web only via server actions.
- New tables: RLS on, `owner_read`, `pipeline_write`, grants to `pipeline_rw` (pattern of `0006_price_history.sql`). Retention 400 days.
- Python tests via `pipeline/scripts/in-docker.sh`; `ruff check .` passes; LF line endings.
- Copy (web): standing notes "Not financial advice. Nothing is ordered: you place trades yourself.", "Frequent day trading in a TFSA can be treated as a business, which makes the gains taxable (not tax advice).", "Yahoo's free 5-minute bars can lag a few minutes."; no-trade line "No signal today has a positive record after costs ({n} signals checked)."; late line "Late: the price has moved since the signal; entering now is a different trade."; track record label "Paper: what the rule's top-3 trades would have done, after costs."

## Review Focus

1. A TSX symbol (e.g. `RY.TO`) and a TSX holiday when NYSE is open (and the reverse): the scan and the guard must use each market's own calendar.
2. A 13:00 early close: cutoffs 12:30 / 12:55, and the morning job must not wait for bars that never come.
3. A stock whose 9:30–9:40 bars are incomplete (halt, thin): not usable, no signal, no crash.
4. Yahoo answering 429 or nothing for a whole cycle: the cycle records the error and the next cycle runs; the page shows the last update time.
5. A signal whose stop is at or above its entry, or whose stop is under one cent away: rejected by the plan, never shown.
